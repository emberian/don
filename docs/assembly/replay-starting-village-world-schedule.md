# Starting-Village World mask and first-strategy schedule

This tranche owns the World-side `TData::CITY` mutation produced while the starting
Village is activated.  It does **not** reimplement `Build::activate`; the separate
activation owner supplies a typed `WallMaskCityRequest` containing the canonical Build
identity, decoded tile center, resolved radius, install argument, native `0x23` flags and
`city == -1`, plus normalized post-activation `0x27` flags.  Evidence here is the supported
`ron-bin/riseofnations.exe`, its
GUID-matched `rise.pdb`, and Capstone instruction reads.  Replay checksums are comparison
outputs only.

## Exact writer chain

`Setup::build_cities` calls the Build virtual initializer at `0x005aba15`, then
`Build::activate(0,0,0)` at `0x005ababf`.  The ordinary fresh center reaches:

```text
Build::activate                    0x00623e20
  Wall::activate                  call 0x00623f65
    Build::start                  dispatch 0x0063e538
      Wall::start
        Wall::mask_me(1, 3)       call 0x0063e86e
          BuildType::mask_me      call 0x0064305f
            is-City late arm      0x0063176d..0x006317c9
              Wall::mask_city     call 0x006317c9
                TData |= 0x0100   0x0063e36a..0x0063e36f
```

The CITY write is not the rectangular blocker loop at `0x00631450..0x0063163d`.
That earlier part of the same `BuildType::mask_me` call clears STARTED/STARTED2, stamps
blocker kind 3, and updates road/blocked/bad rollups.  CITY is a later, type-gated call to
`Wall::mask_city`.  That distinction explains why scanning only the obvious footprint loop
falsely reported no CITY producer.

At `Wall::mask_me` `0x00642fcf..0x00643035`, the Build's `city +0x72` is still `-1`.
The function therefore resolves the radius through
`LeaderData::get_radius(BuildType::type)` at `0x00643012`; a nonnegative City link would
instead select `CityData::get_radius` at `0x00643030`.  The City slot is allocated later in
the Build activation body.  The sentinel-to-slot transition is therefore not merely a
checksum scalar: it selects the radius source for the World mutation.

The native mask call occurs after `Wall::start` ORs STARTED at `0x0063e826` but before
`Wall::activate` ORs ACTIVE at `0x0063e53e`, so its contemporaneous ordinary flags are
`0x23`.  The cross-lane request preserves both temporal images: native `0x23` plus
`city == -1`, and normalized post-activation `0x27`.  The World transaction validates all
three before the first staged CITY write.

## The separate even-circle table

`Wall::mask_city` reads:

- `even_circle_x` at `0x00cae710`;
- `even_circle_y` at `0x00cab3b0`; and
- cumulative `even_circle_radius` at `0x00cae600`.

These are generated by `even_circle_init` `0x006816d0`.  They are **not** the ordinary
`circle_init` arrays used by fog and the later WData City census.  Reusing
`borders_fog::CircleTable` here produces the wrong geometry and cell count.

For radius `r = 1..64`, `even_circle_init` walks source `x` outer and `y` inner over
`[-r-1, r+1]`, skipping either zero axis.  It computes binary32 `sqrt(x*x+y*y)`, rounds up
only when the fractional part is strictly greater than `0.5`, and admits the point when
the result equals `r`.  Before storage, each positive coordinate is decremented.  Rings
are appended, so `even_circle_radius[r]` is cumulative.  Radius one is consequently the
ordered four-point set:

```text
(-1,-1), (-1,0), (0,-1), (0,0)
```

Measured cumulative endpoints reproduced by the port are:

| radius | cumulative TData offsets | coordinate extent |
|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 4 | `-1..0` |
| 2 | 12 | `-2..1` |
| 20 | **1232** | `-20..19` |
| 24 | **1788** | `-24..23` |
| 64 | 12828 | `-64..63` |

An ordinary starting Village therefore installs 1,232 CITY tiles when its radius-20 disc
is wholly on-map.  An Indian-bonus radius-24 center installs 1,788.  The 104/144 counts in
the later `Leader::plan_strategy` receipt belong to the much coarser WData census circle
and must not be substituted here.

`Wall::mask_city` tests `ObjectData::flags & 0x20`, then processes every cumulative offset
from zero to the radius endpoint.  Each offset is added to the TCoord center; signed
negative coordinates and coordinates at or beyond `World +0x18/+0x1c` are skipped.
Every admitted entry executes a word OR with `0x0100`, even when the bit was already set.
There are no RNG calls, WData writes, registry writes, or City writes in this leaf.

The typed transaction stages all in-bounds TData indices before mutating the canonical
`map_terrain::World`, so a malformed cross-lane request fails without a partial disc.
The receipt distinguishes native write instructions, newly changed cells, already-set
cells, and edge-clipped offsets.

`StartingSetupState` now consumes this transaction immediately after each exact center
constructor. It revalidates the native `0x23`/`city == -1` image, the normalized `0x27` and
allocated slot, plus the canonical Build identity and position before it publishes the
receipt with `activation_mask_inputs_joined == true`.

The focused gates reconstruct the three admitted two-human recordings (2018-11-17
Himalayas, 2020-02-08 Old World, and 2020-02-21 Himalayas), consume both exact setup
centers and their source-derived radius-20/radius-24 facts, and prove that each whole
even-circle disc is in bounds. They check the post-transaction center bit and then show
that the exact City terrain census advances through its installed-content mode-one gather
owner. The resulting candidate is measured against, but never fit from, retail and remains
0/3. No recorded checksum is an input.

## Setup order before the first census

The broader pre-City base image is already produced before empires are built:

```text
Map::make                     setup call 0x005ac657
Terrain::init                 setup call 0x005ac778
  check_and_set_halfland                0x00852086
  Map::make_rivers                      0x00852693
  Cliffs::init                          0x00852841
Terrain::mark_forest          setup call 0x005acc41
Leader / empire setup         calls      0x005ad40b / 0x005ad4d2
```

Those functions are genuine World/TData producers, not presentation work:
halfland/rivers/cliffs establish the map surface and Region inputs, and `mark_forest`
ORs `BEHIND_B 0x0008` around forest tiles.  They precede the activation CITY mask and are
part of the reconstructed initial-map owner.

Within `Setup::build_cities`, the relevant order after `Build::init` is:

```text
Build::activate(0,0,0)        0x005ababf
Object::update_seen(0)        0x005abae8
Object::update_seen_ally      0x005abb04
World::compute_all_territory  0x005abb25  (after City allocation)
```

Each empire runs `build_cities` before `build_units`.  After all empires, setup calls
`World::analyze_map` at `0x005ad5f4` and unconditionally calls
`World::compute_all_territory` again at `0x005ad603`.  The final pass resets every Region
border cursor and synchronously runs `World::compute_reg_territory` `0x006b0bb0`.  It is
the authoritative pre-strategy producer for WData `who/who2` and City `bordering +0x65`:
the latter is cleared at `0x006b0c40` and receives competing-owner bits at
`0x006b17c8..0x006b17ec`.

The first frame does not repair a missing setup image before strategy:

```text
Game::do_frame
  Leaders::strategy_all       call 0x00592448
    Leader::plan_strategy     call 0x006ed452, per active Leader
  GameDaemon::process_all     call 0x0059244d
```

All active Leaders finish `check_explore -> plan_strategy -> compute_score -> diplomacy`
before GameDaemon begins.  Thus every first City terrain census observes the same final
setup-produced TData CITY disc and territory image.  GameDaemon's frame-zero border pass
is too late, and there is no daemon refresh interleaved between Leaders.  The general
powers script is frame-positive gated; only a genuinely bound game script is an earlier
general mutation escape hatch, and ordinary admitted replays have none.

## Readiness boundary

This tranche closes one concrete reason the prior source census returned placement grade
zero: `space_at_corner(..., need_city=true)` now observes the exact CITY disc in the
canonical setup World. It does not promote either first-checkpoint checksum image. The
remaining composition must join:

- setup's final full territory result, including City `bordering`;
- the earlier Unit-derived `free`, `busy`, and `peasant_dist` City fields in
  `Leader::plan_strategy`; and
- mounting the now-exact installed `LandData`/`GoodTypeData` mode-one result only after the
  final canonical World and other City owners join; and
- the rest of the activation owner's blocker/road/World rollups needed by other channels.

Accordingly the receipt keeps `first_checksum_world_image_ready == false` and
`first_checksum_city_image_ready == false`.  No recorded checksum is used to fill any
missing field.
